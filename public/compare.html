Earth

(1.00 M⊕ / 1.00 g)

Highly dynamic terrestrial planet featuring a global biosphere, active plate tectonics, and a closed liquid-water hydrological cycle.

Mean Radius 6,371.0 km
Surface Pressure 1,013.25 hPa
Mean Temp +14.4°C (288 K)
Crustal Thickness 7 - 35 km
Atmosphere 78% N₂, 21% O₂
Magnetic Field 0.31 Gauss (Dynamo)

Moon

(0.0123 M⊕ / 0.166 g)

Cooled stagnant-lid rocky satellite lacking a protective atmosphere, shaped by impact cratering and extreme radiative thermal cycles.

Mean Radius 1,737.4 km
Surface Pressure ~10⁻¹⁰ hPa (Exosphere)
Mean Temp ~ -23°C (250 K)
Crustal Thickness 34 - 43 km (GRAIL)
Exosphere He, Ne, ⁴⁰Ar, CO₂
Magnetic Field None (Remanent only)

Mars

(0.107 M⊕ / 0.379 g)

Stagnant-lid red planet with a thin CO₂ atmosphere, global dust storms driving thermodynamics, and complex subsurface hydration cycles.

Mean Radius 3,389.5 km
Surface Pressure 6.1 hPa (Variable)
Mean Temp -63°C (210 K)
Crustal Thickness 49 ± 17 km (InSight)
Atmosphere 95.3% CO₂, 2.6% N₂
Magnetic Field None (Extinct 4 Ga)
